Core Curriculum

Phase 1 — Awaken
Weeks 1–4  ·  Steps 1, 2, 3
She stops calling the discomfort a problem and starts calling it information.
1
Recognize
Recognize

She acknowledges that the restlessness, disconnection, and dissatisfaction are not signs something is wrong with her. They are signs she has outgrown the identity and life she built. This is the permission to begin.

  • The discomfort is not dysfunction — it's data. Restlessness and the quiet feeling that something is off are the nervous system's way of saying: this no longer fits.
  • The life you built made sense for who you were. You didn't do it wrong — you've outgrown the woman who built it, and you've been pretending you haven't.
  • Naming it is the beginning of everything. You cannot change what you won't acknowledge. The moment she says out loud — something is off and I am done pretending it isn't — that's when the work becomes possible.
📋
The Honest Audit
Helps her map her external life against her internal truth so she can see clearly — for the first time — exactly where the gap lives and what it's been costing her.
2
Decode
Decode

She learns her actual design — Human Design and astrology — as a living blueprint for who she is, how she's meant to move, make decisions, and create. She stops measuring herself against someone else's operating system.

  • You have been running someone else's operating system. HD and astrology are not personality tests — they are maps of how she is actually built. When she understands her design, the self-judgment starts to dissolve.
  • Your authority is the most important thing you will ever learn about yourself. Every pattern she has developed has been a workaround for not trusting this signal.
  • Your chart is not a ceiling — it's a compass. Used as a compass rather than a constraint, her blueprint becomes the most liberating thing she has ever encountered.
📋
Her Blueprint Reading — HD + Astrology Personalized Loom
A recorded personalized reading of her Human Design and astrology chart that gives her a living reference point for her authority, energy type, and soul blueprint — something she returns to throughout the entire program.
3
Name
Name

She identifies the specific survival pattern that has been quietly running her decisions — where it came from, what it protected her from, and what it has been costing her. She sees it clearly for the first time without shame.

  • The pattern was brilliant once. The Shapeshifter, the Performer, the Controller, the Ghost, the Strategist — every pattern formed in response to something real, and it worked. That's why it's still here. The work is not to shame the pattern but to graduate from it.
  • The pattern has been making decisions she thought she was making — quietly running her relationships, her career, her creative expression, her sense of self — without her conscious participation.
  • Seeing it clearly is not the same as being defined by it. What has been unconscious can now be chosen. She gets to decide, for the first time, whether she acts from the pattern or from herself.
📋
Her Shadow Story Map
Guides her through naming, tracing, and witnessing her specific survival pattern so she can see exactly how it operates — which rooms it activates, what it does when triggered — and begin to separate herself from it.
📋
Her Season Assessment
Identifies which of the four seasons she is entering and what that season is specifically asking of her — so she can stop fighting her own natural rhythm and start working with it from day one.
What she feels at the end of Phase 1
"I finally understand what has been happening. Nothing is wrong with me. I have been running someone else's operating system inside a life I have already outgrown — and I can see exactly how and why. That awareness alone has already shifted something."
Phase 2 — Unravel
Weeks 5–8  ·  Steps 4, 5, 6
She releases what's past its purpose — without destroying what was valuable.
4
Release
Release

She grieves and releases what is past its purpose — the identity, the roles, the stories, the guilt — and makes the active real-world moves that create space. The garden gets cleared. It looks bare. That's correct.

  • You cannot build the new life inside the old identity. Release is not about destroying what was — it's about completing it. Honoring what it gave her and consciously choosing not to carry it forward.
  • Grief is part of the process and it deserves space. Grief that isn't honored doesn't disappear — it becomes resistance, self-sabotage, or the inability to fully commit to what's next.
  • The void between identities is not a sign she went backward. It is the fertile ground. Nothing can grow in a container that's already full. The clearing is the prerequisite for the creation.
✦
The Release Ritual
A structured process for actively completing what's past its purpose — combining written reflection, somatic awareness, and a closing declaration so the release is felt in the body, not just understood in the mind.
📋
The Desire Inventory
Unedited. Unfiltered. A guided process for naming what she actually wants — without negotiating it down, making it palatable, or asking anyone's permission first.
5
Reclaim
Reclaim

She takes her authority back. Her voice. Her worthiness. Her desires. She learns to trust her own signal — to distinguish fear talking from truth pulling — and returns decision-making back to herself.

  • Worthiness is not something you earn — it's something you remember. She has been operating under a belief that she has to prove her value before she's allowed to want what she wants. Reclaiming worthiness means understanding it was never conditional.
  • Her voice has been edited before it ever left the room. Reclaiming voice is not about becoming louder — it's about stopping the internal approval process that happens before she speaks, creates, decides, or shows up.
  • She has to learn the difference between fear talking and truth pulling. Fear contracts and protects. Truth pulls and expands — even when it's terrifying. Learning to feel that difference in her body is the foundation of genuine self-trust.
📋
Her Abandonment Map
Surfaces the specific moments and patterns where she has been leaving herself — shrinking, editing, deferring — so she can begin reclaiming those moments one by one with intentional, concrete action.
6
Threshold
Cross the Threshold

She makes the decision she has been circling. The one she already knows. She stops protecting the hidden thing and chooses herself — even when it disrupts something. This is the moment the identity shifts.

  • She already knows. The threshold is not about finding more information or waiting for more certainty — it's about acknowledging what she knows and choosing to move from that place instead of continuing to protect the story that keeps her from having to.
  • Nothing that comes into the light holds the same power it had in the dark. When she brings it forward, names it, speaks it, owns it — it loses the grip it has had. Not because everything becomes easy. Because she is no longer spending energy maintaining the hiding.
  • The threshold is the moment the identity actually shifts. Not the insight. Not the breakthrough session. Not the journal entry. The moment she acts on what she knows — that is when the new version of herself becomes real.
📋
The Threshold Letter
The decision named and claimed on paper — a written act of crossing that makes the shift real, witnessed, and impossible to untake. Her permission slip written to herself.
📋
The Courage to Evolve Teaching
Covers the graceful exit, the identity upgrade, and the entry — how to move out of what no longer fits without making it a dramatic destruction, and how to honor both the old and the new in the process.
What she feels at the end of Phase 2
"I have put down what I was carrying. Not destroyed it — completed it. I have said the thing I have been holding back. I have made the decision I have been circling. Something has genuinely shifted — not just in my mind but in my body. I am lighter. And I can feel what's on the other side."
Week 9
Integration Week
No new curriculum. Space to breathe, integrate, and land. Community support channels open. Q&A threads active. This is a deliberate pause built into the architecture — not a gap.
Phase 3 — Root
Weeks 10–12  ·  Steps 5 continued, 7
She builds the internal muscles of self-leadership and aligns with her natural rhythm.
5+
Reclaim
Reclaim — Continued

Step 5 spans both Phase 2 and Phase 3 deliberately. The initial reclaiming happens in Unravel — she names her worthiness, takes her voice off mute, makes the threshold decision. In Phase 3, the self-leadership practice of living from that reclaimed authority deepens. She doesn't just reclaim once. She practices it until it becomes her default.

  • Self-leadership in real time — choosing authenticity over approval in the actual moments it costs something.
  • Returning authority to herself consistently — not once in a breakthrough session, but as a daily practice that compounds over time.
  • The difference between a reclamation that's intellectual and one that's embodied — and how she builds the latter through repetition, not insight.
📋
Her Decision Journal
A daily practice of making decisions from her HD authority in real time — tracking the feel of aligned moves vs. fear moves so she builds the discernment muscle in her body, not just her mind.
7
Align
Align

She learns her season, her cycles, her rhythm — and stops forcing the wrong energy at the wrong time. She understands she is held and supported by something larger than herself. She is not doing this alone.

  • You were never meant to produce at the same level in every season. Understanding her current season — Renewal, Reclaiming, Radiating, or Rebuilding — gives her permission to work with her actual energy instead of against it.
  • The lunar cycle is a monthly rhythm she can learn to work with. When she aligns her creative and emotional energy with the lunar rhythm instead of ignoring it, she stops feeling erratic and starts feeling supported.
  • She is held by something larger than herself — and learning to feel that changes everything. The cocreation framework teaches her she is in a living relationship with her design, the cycles, and universal principles that are always working with her when she is aligned.
📋
Her Seasonal Alignment Guide
Maps what her current season is specifically asking of her — what to lean into, what to stop forcing, and how to honor the natural rhythm she's in rather than fighting it as a personal failure.
📋
Her Lunar Rhythm Map
Her monthly creative and emotional energy mapped to the lunar cycle — a practical tool she keeps and uses every month long after the program ends to stop feeling scattered and start feeling supported.
What she feels at the end of Phase 3
"I know how I am built. I know what season I am in. I know how to make decisions from my own authority instead of everyone else's. I am not doing this alone — and I can feel the difference between forcing and flowing. I am building something I can actually sustain."
Phase 4 — Create
Weeks 13–15  ·  Steps 8, 9
She builds a life that is genuinely, recognizably, unapologetically hers.
8
Create
Create

She builds. Sustainably. From her actual truth, her design, her desires. Not a better-performing version of the old life — something genuinely, recognizably hers.

  • Creation from truth looks completely different from creation from conditioning. Building from her actual truth — her desires, her design, her values — produces something that feels alive instead of just functional.
  • Sustainable creation is rhythmic, not relentless. She builds in seasons, in lunar cycles, in ebbs and flows that are designed into her nature. The rhythm is not inconsistency — it is wisdom.
  • The life she is building is not a project — it is an expression. She is not building toward a destination. She is building a life that is a continuous, living expression of who she actually is.
📋
Her Life Architecture Map
A structure for her life, work, and creative expression designed around her actual energy type and design — not inherited from industry norms or what worked for the version of herself she's leaving behind.
📋
Her Ritual System
Daily, weekly, and seasonal practices built specifically for her energy type, season, and design — the sustainable infrastructure of tending to herself that holds the transformation in place.
9
Tend
Tend

She tends to herself — always. Not as a destination but as a practice. Worthiness, voice, movement, rootedness — tended daily. She becomes unshakable. Obsessed with her life. Her way.

  • Tending is not a phase — it is the practice for life. The woman who tends to herself every day, not perfectly but consistently, becomes someone who is genuinely unshakable. Not because nothing moves her. Because she always comes back to herself.
  • The duality is the point — always becoming and already enough at the same time. Learning to hold both of those truths at once is the most advanced thing she will ever do. And it is available to her right now.
  • An unshakable woman is not one who never doubts — she's one who never abandons herself. She tends. She returns. She chooses herself again. Every time. That is the art of being her. Her way.
📋
Her Embodiment Portrait
A written portrait of the woman she has become — her values, her voice, her non-negotiables, her way of moving — so she has a living document to return to whenever she needs to remember who she is.
📋
Her Tending Practice
The ongoing personal practice that sustains everything — designed around her specific design, season, and rhythm so it's actually something she'll return to, not something she abandons when life gets busy.
What she feels at the end of Phase 4
"I am building something real. Something that is mine. Not a better-performing version of the old life — something that feels alive, aligned, and completely obsessed-over. I know how to tend to myself now. I know my rhythm. I know my design. I know my season. And I know I will never fully arrive — and that is not a problem. That is the art of being me."

Playbooks

Ongoing reference tools — designed to be kept, returned to, and used long after the program ends. These are not one-time worksheets. They are your living operating manual.

The Return to Self Reset
Objective — Immediate Reorientation

For the moment she loses herself — in a season, a spiral, or someone else's energy. This is the tool she reaches for to find her way back to herself without having to start over from scratch.

The Season Map
Objective — Seasonal Clarity + Permission

Helps her identify which season she's in, what it's asking, what to stop forcing, and how to work with her natural energy instead of against it. The practical backbone of the whole philosophy.

The Lunar Living Guide
Objective — Monthly Rhythm + Cocreation

Her monthly creative and emotional energy mapped to the lunar cycle so she stops feeling erratic and starts feeling supported. She uses this one every single month — long after the program ends.

The Pattern Interruption Guide
Objective — In-the-Moment Pattern Break

A practical in-the-moment tool for when the Shapeshifter starts adjusting, the Controller starts gripping, the Ghost starts dimming. Not a concept — something she can reach for right then, in real time.
